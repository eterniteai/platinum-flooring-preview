FAQ

Common questions

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes — we offer free in-home measurements and estimates across Severn, Glen Burnie and the Greater Baltimore area. Call (443) 323-1895 to schedule.

What types of flooring do you install?

Carpet, hardwood, luxury vinyl plank (LVP), luxury vinyl tile, sheet vinyl and laminate. We also offer hardwood refinishing to bring existing floors back to life.

How long does a typical installation take?

Most residential rooms are completed in 1–2 days. Whole-home projects and hardwood refinishing typically take 3–5 days depending on square footage and finish cure times.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Platinum Flooring LLC is a licensed and insured Maryland flooring contractor, serving homeowners and businesses since 1999.

Do you handle commercial projects?

Yes — we manage both residential and commercial flooring projects end-to-end, from product selection at our Severn showroom to final installation.
